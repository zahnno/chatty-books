The problem

Every book is a one-way conversation.

Readers

finish a book with questions only its characters could answer, and nobody to ask.

Authors

get paid once, at the sale. Readers keep living in their world, and indie authors have no way to earn from that.

The product

A reading app where the characters talk back.

Read in a full e-reader or listen to the audiobook. Talk to any character by text or voice, grounded in the page you're on. See scenes illustrated by AI.

Live on the web, Google Play and the Solana dApp Store. iOS is in App Store review.

Trust, safety and polish

Open publishing, with guardrails.

1

AI screening flags famous copyrighted works and harmful content before anyone reads it.

2

Human approval on an admin page; support is emailed for every submission.

3

Live edits reviewed: holders edit the cast; every change is AI-checked, logged and revertible.

4

Clear errors before the wallet opens, like a low SKR balance.

Architecture

No Solana SDK. The wallet only signs.

Seeker app

Expo / React Native

Mobile Wallet Adapter: sign in, sign-and-send for passes, sign-only for co-signed mints. Seed Vault signs.

⇄
API · Node

Chatty Books server

Builds every transaction and verifies every signature with hand-written encoding. Prices SKR live, co-signs the NFT, checks fees on-chain, tracks earnings per character, pays claims.

⇄
Solana mainnet

On-chain

Metaplex Core NFTs, SPL transfers in SKR and USDC, treasury, capped payout wallet.

AI

Character chat and voice, book screening, edit screening.

Review

Admin web page, support emails, one-click revert.

Data

MongoDB: books, characters, earnings ledger, payouts.

Chatty Books · Solana Seeker Hackathon9

Early traction, honestly

Readers who try it keep talking. Authors asked first.

Readers

44%

of 84 sign-ups chatted with a character; 59% of those sent 5+ messages and half talked to 2+ characters.

Authors

3

unsolicited author inquiries asking to publish, before Publish on Solana existed. Two books are now minted.

Growth

16

sign-ups in Sept–Oct since the mobile launch, out of 84 total since Feb 2025. 135 books, 735 characters.

Still early: 22% of chatters came back on another day and nobody pays yet outside our own tests. First users we're going after: Seeker owners who read (wallet and SKR already in hand) and indie authors who want a second income stream from their readers. Internal and test accounts excluded.

Next: a live demo at a book fair alongside a games convention in Thailand, signing up readers and our first community authors.
